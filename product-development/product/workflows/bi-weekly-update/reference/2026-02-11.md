# February 2026: What the Team Is Building Now

example_product has three objectives this quarter: (1) raise extraction quality to production-grade for complex, multi-step workflows, (2) build the enterprise features needed to convert pilot customers, and (3) expand platform integrations so processed documents fit into customers' existing stacks.

## Extraction Quality (OKR 1)

**Goal:** 80% of automation runs pass production readiness review without manual field correction

Enterprise pilots have surfaced that single-form extraction is table stakes. Meridian and CloudKitchen both need multi-step workflows with shared routing state, signer authentication, and mobile-friendly forms. This OKR targets the gap between "impressive demo" and "actually publishable."

| Workstream | Why It Matters | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Multi-Step Workflow Building** | Pilots need full intake-to-signature workflows, not isolated forms | Engineering: core routing + step sequencing system in review. Target: Feb 28 |
| **Clause & Field Block Library** | Enterprise teams bring their own contract clauses and field sets | Design complete. Engineering starting sprint 7 |
| **Conditional Routing Logic** | Complex approval chains need predictable branching | Research phase. Evaluating rule-engine patterns for multi-approver workflows |
| **Brand & Template Customization** | Customers need brand consistency across published portals | Spec complete. Blocked on clause & field block library work |
| **Mobile-Friendly Intake Forms** | 40% of pilot signers submit from mobile devices | Shipped v1 in Jan. Iterating on edge cases from Meridian feedback |

## Enterprise & Publishing (OKR 2)

**Goal:** Close 3 enterprise pilots with $50K+ ARR each

Every enterprise prospect asks about SSO, audit logging, and team workspaces in the first call. These are hard blockers for procurement approval.

| Workstream | Why It Matters | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **SSO / SAML Integration** | Hard requirement for Meridian (healthcare compliance) | Engineering: 60% complete. SAML flow working, SCIM provisioning next |
| **Audit Logging** | Nexus Financial requires full audit trail for compliance | Spec complete. Engineering starting sprint 8 |
| **Team Workspaces** | Collaboration is the #1 request from teams > 5 people | Design in progress. User research with CloudKitchen scheduled for Feb 14 |
| **Approval Routing Engine** | Customers want documents routed through their existing approval chains | Shipped Slack notification integration. Email escalation rules in sprint 7 |
| **Environment Management** | Enterprise teams need staging/production portal separation | Early design phase. Gathering requirements from pilots |

## Platform & Integrations (OKR 3)

**Goal:** Support the top 3 business systems and top 2 identity providers natively

Automation runs that require manual data entry into other systems create friction that kills conversion. If example_product extracts a document's data, it needs to deliver that data everywhere it's needed.

| Workstream | Why It Matters | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **E-Signature Provider Integration** | Every workflow needs a compliant signature capture step | DocuSign shipped. Adobe Acrobat Sign in sprint 7. Dropbox Sign deprioritized |
| **Business System Connectors** | Full workflow automation requires pushing extracted data downstream | Salesforce shipped. NetSuite connector in review. QuickBooks next |
| **Identity Provider Support** | SSO is the first integration every enterprise account needs | Okta shipped. Azure AD in development. OneLogin planned |
| **Plugin / Extension API** | Power users want to extend extraction with custom logic | Early research. Gathering requirements from Aether Logistics |
| **Document Storage Export** | Enterprise teams archive completed documents in their own infrastructure | AWS S3 export in beta. GCP and Azure Blob scoped for Q2 |

## Designs Complete (Ready for Engineering)

- Team workspace invite flow (pending clause & field block library)
- Audit log viewer and search UI
- Multi-step workflow navigation patterns (progress bar, section navigation, save-and-resume)

## What We're Hearing

**Enterprise buyers are converting on quality, blocking on compliance.** The extraction quality improvements shipped in January are landing well with enterprise prospects. Meridian expanded their pilot from 3 to 8 document workflows after seeing the mobile-friendly form improvements, and Nexus Financial's operations lead called the extraction output "surprisingly production-ready." But compliance features (SSO, audit logging) remain hard blockers for procurement.

- *Compliance as gatekeeper:* Meridian's CISO requires SAML SSO before any workflow touches patient-adjacent data. Nexus Financial's compliance team needs full audit trails. These aren't nice-to-haves.
- *Collaboration as accelerator:* CloudKitchen and Aether both highlighted that their teams build workflows in isolation and then struggle to share and refine them together. Team workspaces could be the unlock for multi-seat expansion.

**Free-tier users are our best competitive intelligence source.** TrueNorth Analytics switched from Dropbox Sign because Dropbox Sign handles signatures but not upstream extraction or routing. Their feedback on storage export friction directly validates our document storage export roadmap.

- *Delivery is the new battleground:* Dropbox Sign and PandaDoc both stop at signature collection. Our approval routing engine and document storage export work is a meaningful differentiator.

## Highlights

1. **Meridian Health expanding pilot 2.5x.** Their team went from 3 to 8 document workflows on example_product, driven by mobile-friendly form improvements. SSO remains the single blocker for enterprise-wide rollout.
2. **Nexus Financial compliance team is the buyer.** Operations loves the product but procurement won't approve without audit logging. Sprint 8 start aligns with their March evaluation deadline.
3. **CloudKitchen: collaboration is the unlock for multi-seat.** Their 8-person team currently builds workflows individually and shares via Slack. Team workspaces could convert them from 2 seats to 8.
4. **Dropbox Sign defection validates full-workflow strategy.** TrueNorth Analytics switched to example_product specifically because Dropbox Sign is signature-only. First concrete evidence that our full-workflow approach wins in head-to-head.
5. **AWS storage export in beta is drawing enterprise interest.** Aether Logistics and TrueNorth both independently asked about exporting completed documents to their own infrastructure. Document storage export is higher priority than we estimated.

## Emerging Product Direction

The enterprise buyer ICP is becoming clearer: mid-size operations teams (8-20 people) at companies where document processing is a tax on back-office time. These teams don't want to hire dedicated document-ops specialists. They want to automate paperwork from a template and refine it quickly.

The conversion path is: ops lead finds example_product -> solo pilot (1-2 seats) -> proves ROI on 3-5 workflows -> pushes for team adoption -> compliance/management approves -> full team rollout (8-20 seats).

# Meridian Health: Pilot Exec Summary

**Company:** Healthcare SaaS platform, 200 employees, Series C
**Team:** 12-person operations team, 4 currently on example_product
**Pilot started:** December 2025
**Pilot scope:** Patient intake form automation for clinical operations team
**Status:** Expanding from 3 to 8 workflows. SSO is the blocker for enterprise-wide rollout.

## Pain Points

1. **Document processing is a tax on clinical operations.** Meridian's ops team spends ~30% of time manually keying in patient intake forms for clinical operations. Every form is hand-processed by a different staffer, and they have 15+ intake document types.
2. **Brand and UX inconsistency.** Each published intake portal was set up by a different staffer with different patterns. Clinical staff complain about the learning curve.
3. **Compliance gates slow everything down.** Any workflow touching patient-adjacent data requires CISO approval. The approval process takes 4-6 weeks per workflow.

## example_product Use Cases Today

- **Clinical scheduling intake form:** Automated full intake-to-routing workflow for managing provider schedules. Replaced a 3-month internal project.
- **Patient feedback extraction:** Automated a read-only summary pulling structured fields from their survey PDFs. Operations team uses it daily.
- **Inventory request tracker:** Supply chain team requested a purchase-order intake workflow. Built in 2 hours, previously estimated at 2 sprints.

## Feature Gaps

| Gap | Impact | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| SSO / SAML | Blocker for enterprise rollout | *"My CISO won't approve anything without SAML. Period."* - VP of Engineering |
| Clause & field block library support | Inconsistent branding across published portals | *"We need these to look like Meridian forms, not generic templates."* - Lead Engineer |
| On-prem export | HIPAA-adjacent compliance | *"Patient data can never touch a third-party server."* - CISO |

# CloudKitchen: Pilot Exec Summary

**Company:** Restaurant operations platform, 80 employees, Series B
**Team:** 8-person operations team, 2 currently on example_product
**Pilot started:** January 2026
**Pilot scope:** Vendor invoice and supplier contract automation for restaurant operations
**Status:** Strong individual usage. Team workspaces needed for broader adoption.

## Pain Points

1. **Invoice sprawl.** CloudKitchen has 20+ vendor invoice formats processed with different tools (Retool, manual entry, spreadsheets). No consistency, hard to maintain.
2. **Collaboration friction.** Staff build and run workflows individually and share screenshots in Slack. No way to hand off, review, or refine together.
3. **Real-time data requirements.** Restaurant ops requires live data (order volume, kitchen times, driver ETAs) alongside extracted invoice totals. Most document tools can't handle live connections.

## example_product Use Cases Today

- **Vendor invoice extraction workflow:** Real-time view of invoice totals matched to purchase orders by restaurant. Built with a live connection to their API.
- **Supplier contract intake analyzer:** Comparative pricing extraction pulling terms from multiple supplier contracts. Product team uses it for pricing decisions.

## Feature Gaps

| Gap | Impact | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| Team workspaces | Blocker for team adoption | *"I love this tool but I can't share what I build with my team."* - Senior Engineer |
| Custom data connectors | Need proprietary API support | *"We need to plug in our restaurant management API, not just generic REST."* - CTO |
| Mobile-first templates | Ops managers use tablets in kitchens | *"Half our users are on iPads in restaurant kitchens."* - VP of Operations |
