## Introducing One-Click Publish

Starting today, every example_product workflow includes a **Publish** button that takes your workflow from draft to a live signing portal in under 5 minutes.

### How it works

1. **Click Publish** in any example_product workflow
2. **Choose your delivery** - branded portal, email, or Salesforce
3. **Configure your environment** - custom domain, notification settings, signer roles
4. **Review and confirm** - we show you exactly what will go live
5. **You're live** - your workflow gets a portal URL, SSL certificate, and a monitoring dashboard

### What's included

- **E-signature built in** - every published workflow generates a compliant, auditable signature flow you own
- **Auto-scaling** - your portal scales with submission volume automatically
- **Rollback** - one-click rollback to any previous published version
- **Environment management** - staging and production portals with promotion workflows
- **Custom domains** - connect your own domain with automatic SSL provisioning
- **Monitoring** - built-in health checks, uptime monitoring, and error alerting
