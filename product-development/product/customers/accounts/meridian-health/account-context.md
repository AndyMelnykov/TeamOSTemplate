# Meridian Health - Account Context

## What They Use example_product For

Healthcare technology company with a 35-person engineering team. Uses example_product to automate patient intake forms and pre-visit questionnaires — extracting demographic and insurance information and routing clinical intake workflows to staff for review. Running 22 active workflows - one of the highest workflow counts across all accounts.

## Goals and Success Criteria

- Scale example_product usage across additional clinical teams
- Achieve HIPAA compliance certification for example_product-published workflows
- Reduce intake-workflow-to-published cycle time
