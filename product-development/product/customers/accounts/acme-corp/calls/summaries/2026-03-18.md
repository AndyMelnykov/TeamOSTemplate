## Company Context

Acme Corp is a 50-person B2B SaaS startup with 12 team members using example_product across 8 active workflows, averaging 35 automation runs per week. They adopted example_product 6 months ago for internal document workflow automation and have published 5 workflows to production to date. Champion is Derek Liu (VP Engineering).

## Key Themes

### 1. Version Rollback Reliability (Pain Point — P0)

Acme experienced a production incident on March 13 where a team member attempted to roll back a workflow to a previous version, and the rollback silently dropped three required approval steps from the routing configuration. The team did not discover the missing approval steps until 40 minutes later, when a vendor contract reached e-signature without legal or finance sign-off. Derek described this as a trust-breaking moment — if rollback is unreliable, they cannot use example_product for production paperwork with confidence. He was visibly frustrated and emphasized that version history "can't just be a nice-to-have — it needs to be rock solid." Jordan acknowledged the severity and committed to investigating the root cause.

### 2. Version Diff Visibility (Pain Point — P1)

Priya flagged that the current version history UI shows a list of timestamps but provides no visibility into what actually changed between versions. Without a diff view, team members are "rolling back blind" — they cannot assess the impact of a rollback before executing it. Priya requested a visual diff similar to GitHub's PR diff view, showing added, removed, and modified steps and fields across the whole workflow. She noted that a proper diff view would have prevented the March 13 incident, since the team member would have seen the missing approval steps before confirming the rollback.

### 3. One-Click Publish Beta Progress (Positive — P1)

On a positive note, Acme has been actively using the One-Click Publish beta and is very satisfied with the experience. They published two additional workflows this month — an internal expense-approval form and a customer reference request form — and Derek described the process as taking "minutes instead of days." This is a significant improvement over their previous workflow of manually configuring approval routing and signature integration. Derek said the publishing improvements are "the kind of thing that gets the whole team excited" and credited it with increasing overall example_product enthusiasm across the team.
