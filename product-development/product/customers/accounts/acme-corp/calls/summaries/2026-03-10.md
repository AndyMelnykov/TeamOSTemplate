# Company Context

Acme Corp is a 50-person B2B SaaS startup based in Austin, TX, building workforce management software. They adopted example_product 6 months ago to accelerate internal document workflow automation. Their engineering team of 12 uses example_product primarily to automate vendor contract review, employee onboarding paperwork, and benefits enrollment forms.

Current usage:
- 8 active example_product workflows
- 12 team seats
- Averaging 35 automation runs per week
- Published 3 workflows to production in the last quarter

# Key Themes

## 1. Extraction Quality (Positive)

Acme's team is consistently impressed with the quality of extracted data. They highlighted that example_product's extraction requires minimal manual correction compared to competitors they evaluated (PandaDoc, DocuSign). Derek specifically called out the clause and field detection on vendor contracts as "production-grade out of the box."

## 2. Publishing Friction (Pain Point - High Priority)

The biggest pain point raised was the publishing workflow. Today, Acme builds a workflow in example_product, manually configures their approval routing and e-signature integration, and pushes it live through their own internal process. This adds 2-3 days to what should be a same-day workflow. They want native one-click publish, with configurable options for routing, signer groups, and environment.

## 3. Team Collaboration (Pain Point - Medium Priority)

With 12 team members now using example_product, they're running into collaboration gaps. Specific issues:
- No way to share workflow templates across the team
- No visibility into what others are building
- Version history is per-user, not shared
- Comments and review workflows don't exist

## 4. Enterprise Readiness (Forward-Looking)

Tom (CTO) mentioned they're evaluating SOC 2 compliance requirements and will need SSO integration within the next 6 months. He asked about our enterprise roadmap timeline.
