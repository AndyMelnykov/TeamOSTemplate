# Acme Corp - Account Context

## What They Use example_product For

50-person B2B SaaS startup in Austin building workforce management software. Engineering team of 12 owns internal tooling and uses example_product to automate vendor contract review and employee onboarding paperwork — extracting key terms from vendor agreements and routing new-hire forms (offer letters, I-9s, benefits enrollment) through approval and e-signature. Currently running 8 active workflows with 35 automation runs per week and 3 workflows published last quarter.

## Goals and Success Criteria

- Accelerate internal document workflow automation (their primary use case)
- Expand example_product usage from ad hoc contract review into full onboarding workflows
- Add 8 more seats to cover the full engineering team

## Relationship Context

- **Champion:** Derek Liu (VP Engineering) - consistently positive about extraction quality, actively advocates for example_product internally
- **Power user:** Priya Nair (Lead Developer) - provides detailed technical feedback, wants shared templates and collaboration features
- **Executive sponsor:** Tom Beckett (CTO) - involved in QBRs, focused on ROI and team productivity

## Open Risks and Blockers

- Publishing friction is their biggest pain point - drafted 6 onboarding workflows last quarter but only published 2 because of approval-routing setup overhead
- Wants collaboration features (shared templates, workflow visibility) - two team members independently built nearly identical onboarding forms due to no visibility
- Renewal is June 2026 with strong expansion signal, but publishing improvements are needed to unlock full team adoption
