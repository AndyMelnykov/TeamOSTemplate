# Helix Robotics - Account Context

## What They Use example_product For

Robotics company with a 15-person team. Uses example_product to automate purchase orders and supplier contracts for their hardware components — extracting line-item pricing and delivery terms from supplier POs and routing master service agreements through legal review. Running 11 active workflows.

## Goals and Success Criteria

- Expand example_product usage to supplier onboarding and vendor risk assessment workflows
- Native ERP integration for extracted purchase order data (currently limited to CSV/PDF export)
- Supplier document templates for common hardware component categories
