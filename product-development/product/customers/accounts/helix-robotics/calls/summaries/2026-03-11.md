## Company Context

Helix Robotics is a robotics company with a 15-person team and 11 active example_product workflows. They use example_product primarily to automate purchase orders and supplier contracts for their hardware components — extracting pricing, delivery terms, and compliance clauses from supplier paperwork. Their team is distributed across Austin, Munich, and Shenzhen.

## Key Themes

### 1. Real-Time Collaboration (Pain Point — P0)

Helix's supplier contract and purchase order review process is fundamentally bottlenecked by the lack of real-time collaboration in example_product. Rachel described their current review meetings as "chaos" — team members across three time zones join a call, take turns screen-sharing their workflow edits, and iterate sequentially. A review session that should take 30 minutes stretches to 90+ because only one person can drive at a time. Rachel stressed that async commenting is not a viable workaround; their review cadence requires synchronous, multi-user editing with live cursor visibility. "Our contract review meetings are chaos," Rachel said. "I need live cursors, live editing, everyone in the same canvas at the same time."

### 2. Serialized Editing Workflow (Pain Point — P1)

Ben expanded on the collaboration pain by describing how his workflow with Rachel is completely serialized. When building a supplier contract workflow, Ben handles the extraction rules for pricing and delivery terms while Rachel focuses on the approval routing and legal review logic. Today, they have to wait for each other to finish before reviewing or iterating. Ben estimated this serialization doubles their review cycle time and creates unnecessary context-switching overhead. He wants the ability to work on separate parts of the same workflow simultaneously.

### 3. Native ERP Integration (Feature Request — P1)

Rachel raised interest in native ERP integration for extracted purchase order data. Several of Helix's procurement systems run on NetSuite, and the ability to push extracted PO line items directly into NetSuite alongside their example_product workflows would let them close the loop without manual re-entry. This is not blocking current usage but would expand their example_product footprint significantly.
