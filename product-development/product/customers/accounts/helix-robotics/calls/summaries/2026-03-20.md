## Company Context

Helix Robotics is a robotics company with 15 people across Austin, Munich, and Shenzhen. They have 11 active example_product workflows, primarily purchase order and supplier contract automation. This call was a follow-up to the March 11 check-in where real-time collaboration was identified as their P0 need.

## Key Themes

### 1. Real-Time Collaboration Prototype Demo (Positive — P0)

Sam demoed an early prototype of example_product's real-time collaboration feature, including live cursor tracking, simultaneous multi-user editing, and basic presence indicators. Rachel's reaction was immediate and emphatic — she called it "the thing that makes example_product irreplaceable for us." The demo showed two users editing the same workflow with ~200ms latency on cursor movements and edits propagating in real time. Rachel specifically validated that this would transform their cross-timezone supplier contract review sessions. Ben noted he'd been "waiting for this since we signed up."

### 2. Cursor Visibility and Presence UX (Feedback — P1)

Lena Zhao provided detailed UX feedback on the cursor and presence system. She requested that cursors display user avatars or initials rather than just colored dots, and that cursors indicate which specific field or step a user is actively editing. She described a scenario where she wants to see "Rachel's cursor on the routing step while I'm working on the extraction rules" — step-level awareness, not just page-level. Taylor Brooks discussed approaches including a sidebar presence panel showing each user's active step and selection state.

### 3. Conflict Resolution for Simultaneous Edits (Feedback — P1)

The team discussed edge cases around conflict resolution when two users edit the same workflow step simultaneously. Sam explained the CRDT-based approach, but Rachel flagged that for their use case, they'd want a visual indicator when two people are editing the same step — not a hard lock, but an awareness signal. Lena suggested a subtle highlight or border color on steps being actively edited by another user, similar to how Google Docs highlights active paragraphs.

### 4. Portal Branding Sync (Feature Request — P2)

Lena raised the idea of syncing brand assets from Figma into example_product workflows. Helix maintains a brand kit in Figma with defined colors, logo lockups, and typography. Today, they manually re-upload these for every published document portal. Lena asked whether example_product could import Figma brand tokens as workflow-level configuration, so every published portal automatically uses their brand system. Taylor expressed interest in exploring this as part of the broader design-developer handoff workflow.
