## Company Context

Pinnacle Media is a media company with a 9-person team running 7 active workflows on example_product, automating licensing and rights contracts for footage, music, and talent. Their workflow is draft-heavy, originating in Word/PDF licensing templates before moving to workflow configuration. Renewal is May 2026, and Leo Martinez (champion) is actively evaluating competitors, making this the most likely account to churn in the next 90 days.

## Key Themes

### 1. Contract Template Import Gap (Pain Point — P0)

The single biggest risk to this account. Sofia Huang spends her entire workweek drafting meticulously structured licensing agreements in Word, and the handoff to workflow configuration requires engineers to manually re-key every clause. Leo has been evaluating PandaDoc for the past three weeks specifically because of their template import capabilities, though he was candid that "their extraction quality is garbage compared to example_product." Sofia framed the gap clearly: "If I could drop one of our licensing templates into example_product and get a workflow that matches every clause, that would eliminate the biggest bottleneck on our team." This is a blocking issue for renewal — without a credible timeline on template import, we are likely to lose this account.

### 2. Performance at Scale (Pain Point — P0)

Pinnacle built a rights and licensing contract catalog in example_product that works well with small datasets but degrades badly at production scale. Their contract catalog has 12,000 records, and the app becomes unusable because generated code defaults to client-side filtering. Leo specifically called out the need for virtual scrolling and server-side filtering. He acknowledged the tool "handles 500 contracts beautifully" but said the gap between prototype and production performance is a real blocker for expanding example_product usage to more workflows.

### 3. Template Flexibility for Media Use Cases (Pain Point — P1)

The template customizer doesn't accommodate media-specific contract patterns. Sofia needs music sync license clauses, footage usage-rights clauses with territory and window restrictions, and talent release forms that aren't available in the current template library. Taylor Brooks took detailed notes and acknowledged this as a gap in our vertical coverage. Sofia was complimentary about example_product's baseline publishing quality — "the published contracts actually look good" — but needs more flexibility for media-specific clause patterns.

### 4. Extraction and Publishing Quality (Positive)

Despite the frustrations, both Leo and Sofia remain genuinely impressed with the quality of example_product's output. Leo called it out as the primary reason he wants to stay. Sofia praised the clause hierarchy, clean branding, and proper spacing in published contract portals, noting that "other automation tools generate portals that look like an engineer designed them." This is our strongest retention lever — the quality gap between example_product and competitors is real and the customer sees it clearly.
