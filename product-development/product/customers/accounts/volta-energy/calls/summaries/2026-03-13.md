## Company Context

Volta Energy is a renewable energy company with an 8-person engineering team running 6 active workflows on example_product. They use example_product primarily for field-service work order and compliance-form automation — equipment inspection checklists, site configuration forms, and field operations paperwork. They built 6 workflows last quarter but only published 2, primarily due to site-configuration overhead.

## Key Themes

### 1. Inline Field Mapping Breaks on Nested Forms (Pain Point — P1)

Carlos built an equipment inspection work order with 6 nested checklist sections arranged in a complex form-within-form layout. When he attempts to inline-edit a single field mapping, the layout of the surrounding sections breaks — checklist blocks resize, spacing collapses, and section alignment shifts. He's found a workaround using section decomposition (editing each checklist block as an isolated component and then reassembling), but it's fragile and time-consuming. As Carlos put it: "I built an equipment inspection work order with 6 nested checklist sections. When I try to inline-edit one field mapping, the layout of the other 5 breaks. I end up rebuilding the whole form and losing my tweaks." Jordan confirmed this is a known limitation of the inline field-mapping engine's diffing approach and discussed potential fixes.

### 2. Site/Environment Variable Management for Multi-Site Publishing (Pain Point — P1)

Volta publishes identical work order workflows to 8 different energy sites, each with unique inspector credentials, regional compliance codes, and site-specific configuration parameters. Nina's team currently manages this by manually editing config files before each publish — a process that's both error-prone and unscalable. Nina described it plainly: "We publish the same work order workflow to 8 different energy sites. Each site has different inspector credentials and compliance codes. Right now we're manually editing config files for each publish. It's begging for a site variable system." This is blocking them from publishing more workflows to production — it's a key reason only 2 of their 6 built workflows have shipped.

### 3. Team Workspace Templates (Nice-to-Have — P2)

Nina mentioned that her team frequently starts new workflows from similar foundations — a work order layout with their standard checklist blocks, Volta branding, and common site-data connectors. She'd like the ability to save a workflow as a team template that any team member can instantiate. Lower priority than the field-mapping and publishing issues, but would improve team velocity.
