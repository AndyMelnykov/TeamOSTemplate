## Company Context

Axiom Logistics is a national logistics and supply chain company with a 28-person engineering team running 16 active workflows on example_product. They rely heavily on example_product for production-grade document automation — particularly bill-of-lading extraction, customs declaration processing, and warehouse receiving paperwork across their regional operations.

## Key Themes

### 1. API Rate Limiting at Scale (Pain Point — P0)

Axiom's platform team is consistently hitting example_product's API rate limits during peak processing windows. Their bill-of-lading extraction pipeline makes over 500 API calls per minute between 2-4 PM ET when regional warehouse teams across the Eastern seaboard are simultaneously batch-uploading bills of lading and customs paperwork ahead of the daily carrier cutoff. Deepa described it as a daily operational disruption: "We're hitting rate limits every afternoon between 2 and 4 PM when our warehouse teams are all submitting bills of lading ahead of the carrier cutoff. That's exactly when we need example_product most, and it's exactly when it falls over." Morgan confirmed the throttling pattern aligns with what the infra team has observed on their end and committed to investigating dedicated capacity tiers for Enterprise accounts.

### 2. Customs Filing Integration Gaps (Pain Point — P0)

Raj's operations team has blocked the production rollout of 4 new workflows because example_product lacks native integration with CargoWise, their customs and transportation management system. Their entire filing workflow runs through CargoWise, and the team refuses to introduce a manual export/import step outside that system. Raj was direct: "Our operations team refuses to adopt example_product for live customs filings until we can plug it into CargoWise. They don't want another manual step — they want it in the same filing workflow as everything else." This is a concrete blocker to expansion within their organization.

### 3. Extraction Quality for Complex Documents (Positive)

Deepa praised example_product's extraction quality for multi-line bills of lading. Their BOL manifests — which often carry 200+ line items with mixed HS tariff codes and units of measure — were extracted in a single instruction pass with minimal correction. She called it "the best AI extraction we've seen from any tool" and said it saved her team roughly 3 weeks of manual customs data-entry time.
