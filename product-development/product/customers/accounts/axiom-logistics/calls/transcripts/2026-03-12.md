**Deepa Patel:** Let me start with the rate limiting. We're hitting rate limits every afternoon between 2 and 4 PM when our warehouse teams are all submitting bills of lading ahead of the carrier cutoff. That's exactly when we need example_product most, and it's exactly when it falls over.

Our telemetry shows we spike to about 500 API calls per minute during that window.

**Morgan Wu:** 500 per minute — that's well above the current Enterprise tier limit of 200 per minute. I can see why you're getting throttled consistently.

**Raj Malhotra:** And the error handling isn't great on our end when the throttling kicks in. We get 429s with a generic retry-after header, but our submission UI just shows a spinner and eventually times out. Our users don't know what's happening — they just think example_product is down.
