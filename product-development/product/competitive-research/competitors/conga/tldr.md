# Conga — TL;DR

## Overview
Enterprise document automation platform with deep Salesforce ecosystem integration. Leverages CRM record data to auto-populate and route contracts, quotes, and orders.

## Key Strengths
- Deep integration with Salesforce (and broader CRM/ERP data merge for extraction and routing)
- Enterprise credibility through long-standing presence in regulated industries
- SSO, audit logging, SOC 2, SLAs — full enterprise feature set from day one
- Strong clause and field extraction accuracy for structured contract data tied to CRM records

## Key Weaknesses
- Heavily coupled to Salesforce ecosystem — vendor lock-in concerns for non-Salesforce orgs
- Workflow builder UX is dated and has a steep learning curve
- Slow time-to-value — implementations often require partner/consultant involvement
- No custom domain support for published document portals

## ICP Overlap
Enterprise teams already on Salesforce, regulated industries needing deep CRM-tied contract automation. Competes for enterprise buyers where existing Salesforce investment carries weight.

## Pricing
Enterprise-only, custom quoted. No public self-serve tiers.

## example_product Differentiation
example_product is CRM-agnostic and offers a faster, more modern workflow-building experience with a real preview/refinement loop. Conga has stronger enterprise credibility and CRM-tied extraction accuracy but a much slower, more consultant-dependent path to value. Our window to win enterprise accounts is on speed of implementation and workflow builder UX.

## Bottom Line
Biggest enterprise threat where Salesforce is already the system of record. Product is powerful but slow to stand up and rigid outside the Salesforce ecosystem. We need to win on time-to-publish and builder experience before Conga's AI extraction catches up.
