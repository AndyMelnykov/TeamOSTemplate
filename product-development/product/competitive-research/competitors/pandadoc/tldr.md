# PandaDoc — TL;DR

## Overview
AI-assisted document and proposal builder focused on design quality and visual polish, with e-signature and payment collection built in.

## Key Strengths
- Beautiful, branded documents out of the box — strong template design and layout quality
- Appeals to sales and ops teams who care about proposal aesthetics
- One-click publish to a live, brandable document portal with hosting included
- Custom domain support at low price points

## Key Weaknesses
- Extraction is shallow — mostly template field-fill for outbound documents, not true inbound OCR/clause detection on uploaded paperwork
- No staged/preview environment or version rollback beyond basic history
- No enterprise-grade audit logging or SSO below the top tier
- No real-time multiplayer editing on a workflow (comment-based review only)

## ICP Overlap
Sales teams, small ops/legal teams, early-stage companies sending proposals and contracts. Competes for the same "get a polished document out fast" use case as example_product but is weaker on inbound document intake and extraction.

## Pricing
Freemium with per-seat tiers. Starting at ~$19/mo.

## example_product Differentiation
example_product wins on extraction depth (true field/clause detection with confidence scoring on uploaded/scanned documents), workflow branching, and enterprise-readiness. PandaDoc wins on out-of-the-box design polish and outbound proposal speed.

## Bottom Line
Closest direct competitor on the outbound-document side. Strong on the visual layer and e-signature UX, shallow on inbound extraction and enterprise controls. We need to match their design quality while emphasizing our extraction and workflow-building depth.
