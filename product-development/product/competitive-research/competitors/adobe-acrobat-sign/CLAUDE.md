## Context

- **Competitor type:** Adjacent — overlaps with example_product on document workflow and signature but enters from the PDF-editing tool
- **Relationship to example_product:** Different entry point; Acrobat Sign starts from a PDF file already open in Acrobat, example_product starts from an upload into a purpose-built extraction/routing workflow
- **Why it matters:** Enormous installed base of Acrobat/Reader users already inside the Adobe ecosystem; Sign lowers the barrier from "have a PDF" to "get it signed" without leaving Adobe's tools
