# DocuSign — TL;DR

## Overview
Full-stack document workflow and e-signature platform at docusign.com. Speed- and brand-recognition-focused — the default choice for "just get this signed" across every industry.

## Key Strengths
- Fast, familiar signing experience with near-universal signer recognition (lowers signer drop-off)
- One-click publish to a live signing envelope with hosting included
- Refinement without rebuilding the whole envelope from scratch
- Multi-document, multi-page workflow support in a single envelope
- Simple, approachable UX for non-technical senders

## Key Weaknesses
- Extraction is template/field-tag based, not true AI clause or table detection — accuracy drops on unstructured scans
- Limited real-time collaboration (no true multiplayer workflow editing, basic team folders)
- No staged/preview environment for testing a workflow before it goes live
- Limited customization of routing logic (mostly linear signer order, no conditional branching without add-ons)
- Extraction confidence scoring is not exposed to the sender

## ICP Overlap
Solo operators, small ops/legal teams, and brand-conscious mid-market buyers. Similar to PandaDoc's target but more focused on signature volume than proposal design. Competes for the "just get it signed fast" use case.

## Pricing
Freemium with per-seat, usage-capped tiers. Starting at ~$25/mo.

## example_product Differentiation
example_product wins on extraction quality (true field/clause detection with confidence scoring), workflow branching depth, and staged preview environments. DocuSign wins on signer familiarity and raw send-to-sign speed for simple documents.

## Bottom Line
Direct competitor for the SMB segment and the broadest brand recognition in the category. Strong on speed and signer trust, weak on extraction depth and workflow customization. Growing fast in adjacent CLM features — watch their roadmap.
