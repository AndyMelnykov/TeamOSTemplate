# Dropbox Sign — TL;DR

## Overview
Dropbox's lightweight e-signature platform. Narrow but fast — send a document for signature in seconds, tightly integrated with Dropbox file storage.

## Key Strengths
- Best-in-class signature-request speed and simplicity
- Tight integration with Dropbox file storage and sync
- Strong developer API and embedding experience
- Excellent for simple, one-off signature requests
- Good audit trail and certificate of completion per signature

## Key Weaknesses
- No extraction capability whatsoever — pure signature routing, no OCR, field, or clause detection
- No workflow builder — users must assemble routing/approval logic themselves outside the product
- No publishing pipeline beyond a basic signing link
- No enterprise features below the top tier (no SSO, no audit logs)

## ICP Overlap
Small teams and developers needing a lightweight, embeddable signature flow. Competes for the "just get a signature" use case but not the full-app document automation use case.

## Pricing
Free tier with paid plans for higher signature volume. Starting at ~$20/mo.

## example_product Differentiation
example_product automates the full document lifecycle (extraction, routing, approvals, signature, publishing), not just the signature step. Dropbox Sign is a great narrow signature tool but not a competitor for full document workflow automation.

## Bottom Line
Strong in its lane (fast signature requests) but very limited scope. Not a direct threat for full workflow automation. Risk is Dropbox expanding Sign into adjacent document-intake territory.
