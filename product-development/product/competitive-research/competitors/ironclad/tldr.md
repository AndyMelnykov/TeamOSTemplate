# Ironclad — TL;DR

## Overview
Broad contract lifecycle management platform. Full contract workflow from intake through negotiation, e-signature, and searchable repository, with real-time collaborative redlining.

## Key Strengths
- True full-lifecycle contract workflow (intake, approval routing, signature, repository) in one platform
- Real-time collaborative editing and redlining with multiplayer support
- Strong clause-level AI extraction and contract-language detection
- Built-in workflow designer with conditional approval routing
- Good CRM and e-signature integration

## Key Weaknesses
- Narrower document scope — built for contracts specifically, weaker for invoices, intake forms, or purchase orders
- No custom domain support for published/signing portals
- Enterprise-leaning pricing even at the entry paid tier
- Implementation and workflow setup complexity for non-legal teams

## ICP Overlap
Legal and procurement teams, mid-market companies standardizing contract workflows. Less overlap with example_product's broader document-type coverage but competes hard for the "full contract lifecycle" narrative.

## Pricing
No free tier; paid plans scoped to seat count and workflow complexity. Starting at custom quote only.

## example_product Differentiation
example_product wins on breadth of document types (contracts, invoices, intake forms, purchase orders all in one platform), publishing/portal flexibility, and a more accessible builder for non-legal teams. Ironclad wins on collaboration depth (real-time redlining) and being a full contract-specific IDE-like workspace. Different scope but converging on enterprise contract automation.

## Bottom Line
Most feature-complete competitor for contracts specifically, weak on broader document-type coverage and portal publishing. Watch for their push into adjacent document types (invoices, forms).
